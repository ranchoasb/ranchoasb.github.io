About Rancho ASB

Welcome Broncos!

by Luc Chen, 2025-2026 ASB Communications Director

Rancho San Joaquin Middle School’s ASB (Associated Student Body) is a team of hard-working students who help bring entertainment and school spirit to students and families. ASB organizes and manages events collaboratively, demonstrating the importance of teamwork.

Rancho ASB plans and helps at different events and festivals. Traditionally, ASB organizes Tug of War tournaments, Spirit Weeks, and multiple seasonal festivals allowing students to relax from their busy schedules.

Additionally, ASB also advocates for a positive environment. Through various lunchtime activities and other campaigns, ASB functions to encourage respect, kindness, and leadership to create a friendly atmosphere for all students.

Leadership and communication skills are at the heart of ASB’s mission. Building on past experiences, ASB strives to create a fun and spirited campus life for students while attending Rancho. ASB members learn to work with time and budget constraints, which are important skills that will help them prepare for similar challenges in the future.

The ASB team at Rancho San Joaquin Middle School works to support school spirit and to make students’ time at school memorable. Through strong teamwork and communication, members of ASB set examples of leadership, teamwork, and school spirit for future Broncos.
